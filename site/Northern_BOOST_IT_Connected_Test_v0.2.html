Employer Demand

Built from what IT employers are asking for

The six cards below reflect skills employers are asking for in recent Information Technology job postings across Arizona. Explore each one to see how that skill shows up on the job.

Technical knowledge matters, but IT employers are also looking for people who can troubleshoot, communicate, manage workflow, use automation and AI responsibly, think across systems, and protect reliability.

Employer-demand overview.
Recent employer demand shows that IT work depends on both technical knowledge and strong workplace skills. Communication remains one of the largest signals in the dataset, while troubleshooting, workflow management, automation, operations, scalability, and governance all show strong demand. Artificial intelligence also stands out with major growth, showing how quickly the field is evolving. The message is clear: employers need people who can solve problems, communicate clearly, manage the work, use tools responsibly, and think beyond a one-time fix.
Technical knowledge opens doors. Professional judgment makes the technology useful and reliable. Employers are asking for people who can solve problems, communicate, manage workflow, improve processes, and understand the wider system.
About the employer-demand data

Source: Lightcast Q3 2026. The employer-demand signal uses Information Technology and Computer Science job postings from Maricopa, Pinal, and Pima Counties, Jun 2025–Jul 2026. BOOST uses this Arizona posting sample to illustrate skills employers are requesting; it is not presented as a census of every Arizona job posting.

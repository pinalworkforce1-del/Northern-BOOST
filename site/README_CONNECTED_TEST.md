## New Module 4 BUILD check
When the participant selects BUILD, Module 4 now requires a coach-supported Training & Funding Check before creating the final summary. Structured fields include ETPL status, provider/program/credential, program cost, potential ARIZONA@WORK contribution, Pell program/participant eligibility and potential amount, future-ready Workforce Pell fields, grant amount used for the estimate, estimated remaining balance, and funding-plan status.

Pell and Workforce Pell are displayed separately and are not automatically stacked. All funding fields are planning estimates and are not approvals or guarantees.

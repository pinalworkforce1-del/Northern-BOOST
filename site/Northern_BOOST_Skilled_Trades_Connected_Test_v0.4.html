Before you experience the work, let's look at some of the skills employers are asking for in Skilled Trades.

Six-skill overview.
These findings come from recent skilled-trades job postings across the region. Employers are consistently asking for six workplace skills: communication, troubleshooting, detail orientation, safety, reliability and work ethic, and leadership. Technology is changing the work too, with digital and AI-enabled tools appearing more often across skilled-trades occupations. Select each skill below to explore what it looks like on the job. Once you’ve explored all six, you’ll put those skills into practice in the challenge ahead.
Technology is changing the work. Employer postings are also showing a sharp increase in references to artificial intelligence and other digital skills. That does not mean every skilled-trades job requires AI expertise — it signals that diagnostics, planning, maintenance, estimating, documentation, and problem solving are increasingly supported by digital and AI-enabled tools.
About the employer-demand data

Source: Lightcast Q3 2026. The skill-demand signal uses active job postings across 21 Skilled Trades occupations from Pinal, Maricopa, and Pima Counties, Jun 2025–Jul 2026. BOOST uses this Arizona posting sample to illustrate skills employers are requesting; it is not presented as a census of every Arizona job posting.

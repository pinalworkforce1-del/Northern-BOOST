WHAT TO BRING · PINAL-STYLE PREPARATION LIST

Helpful documents and information to gather

This is a preparation checklist, not an eligibility checklist. Your Northern Arizona team will tell you which documents are required for your situation and how they may be verified. You can meet your coach even if you do not have every item.

Photo identification

A government-issued photo ID, if available. Ask staff about acceptable alternatives.

Social Security card, when requested

Bring it for authorized staff to review only if needed under program verification procedures. Do not enter the number here.

Layoff or separation information, if applicable

Any separation or layoff letter, employer notice, or related information you already have.

Résumé and work history

A résumé, prior job titles and dates, duties, certifications, or employer references—whatever you have available.

Income or unemployment information, if applicable

Relevant pay information, benefit notices, or unemployment records, only when requested by the local workforce team.

Address or residency verification, if requested

A recent letter, lease, bill, or another accepted document. The team can explain alternatives.

Education, training, and military records, if relevant

Diplomas, transcripts, credentials, licenses, military documentation, or other records that help describe your background.

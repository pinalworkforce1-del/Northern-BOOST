How to use this map

Complete Discover, Reality Check, Career Mobility, and Decide in order. Module 4 includes the conditional Training & Funding Check when BUILD is selected. Then use an Applied Career Experience to test the work before returning to the Career Coach.

Coach principle: BOOST supports individualized decision-making. The evidence should clarify the conversation, not manufacture a predetermined career or training outcome.

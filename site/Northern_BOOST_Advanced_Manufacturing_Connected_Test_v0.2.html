Before you experience the work, let's look at some of the skills employers are asking for in Advanced Manufacturing.

Eight-skill overview.
These findings come from recent manufacturing job postings across the region. Employers are consistently asking for eight workplace skills: communication, detail orientation, operations, troubleshooting, problem solving, quality control, continuous improvement, and safety. Modern manufacturing brings people, technology, precision, and problem solving together. Select each skill to explore what it looks like on the job. Once you’ve explored all eight, you’ll put those skills into practice in the challenge ahead.
Modern manufacturing combines people, precision, and technology. Digital production systems, automation, CNC, robotics, inspection technology, maintenance software, and AI-enabled tools increasingly support the work — while human judgment, communication, quality, and safety remain essential.
About the employer-demand data

Source: Lightcast Q3 2026. The employer-demand signal uses active Manufacturing & Production job postings from Pinal, Maricopa, and Pima Counties, Jun 2025–Jul 2026. BOOST uses this Arizona posting sample to illustrate skills employers are requesting; it is not presented as a census of every Arizona job posting.

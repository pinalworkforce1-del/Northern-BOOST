Step 1 • Career Mindmap

Imagine work that just feels right.

For a moment, forget whether you are qualified, whether training is required, or whether the exact job is available nearby. What work naturally pulls your attention?

We are not choosing your career yet. We are using your answer to learn what may energize you at work.

Rosie will use the answers you choose to reflect the pattern back to you. You decide whether it sounds right.
Use a job title if one comes to mind, or simply describe the work.
Picture the workday. What activities are happening?
What makes you think, “Yeah, I could see myself doing that”?
People, customers, a team, machines, a community, a business, animals?
Think about the challenge, not the job title.
Go beyond pay. What would make the work matter to you?
What does work need to provide financially?
What schedule would fit your life?
Inside, outside, active, desk, people, independent?
What would you like this work to lead toward?
How much additional training would you realistically consider right now?

Six-skill overview.
These findings come from recent healthcare job postings across the region. Employers are consistently asking for communication, workflow management, compassion, accountability, quality improvement, and problem solving. Select each skill to explore what it looks like on the job. Once you’ve explored all six, you’ll put those skills into practice in the challenge ahead.
Healthcare combines people, coordination, judgment, and care. Digital records, scheduling systems, diagnostic technology, and other tools support the work — while communication, compassion, accountability, quality, and sound problem solving remain essential.
About the employer-demand data

Source: Lightcast Q3 2026. The employer-demand signal uses Healthcare job postings from Pinal, Maricopa, and Pima Counties, Jun 2025–Jul 2026. BOOST uses this Arizona posting sample to illustrate skills employers are requesting; it is not presented as a census of every Arizona job posting.
